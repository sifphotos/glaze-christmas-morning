01 · project brief

the brief.

01

everyone slept over. santa is in every room. nobody asks why.

video first. visionary shoots the film. i direct and grab stills fast in between. the plot beats come first, santa alone and product-only frames are secondary.

christmas morning in a 1966 mid century house. tyler and goichi, jasey and modu, brittni, and an old santa who shows up everywhere, deadpan, and never explains himself. he's the old man from the birthday drop, back for the holidays.

no dialogue. nobody smiles at camera. think gucci campaign seriousness and nadia lee cohen. slow-mo, 2.39 widescreen for the film, full vertical for the cutdowns, matte film with grain and dust.

the rules on set: a bottle, a box, the scrub jar or the logo paper is in every single frame. the oil always reads as absorbs, grip holds, never greasy. the end card is the GLAZE logo and nothing else.

the scrub opens the film now: macro of the hand in the jar, quick hits on legs and arms, then santa stacking boxes, then the oil after the shower, then santa walks into the bathroom. lip oil is in scope too.

lighting is run and gun: on-camera speedlight, candid, nadia lee cohen style, so we move fast between rooms. shooting can go out of order.

  • full film2.39 letterboxed, every room45 – 50s
  • hero cutthe main ad30s
  • cutdownbathroom, den, bar, pull back15s
  • clean ad cutinsert removed, safe to run paid30s
  • postcardsthe social cuts. short 9:16 + 4:5 loops, one moment each8
  • stillsfull scene + close heroes, 4:5 and 9:16~25
  • product heroesshot inside their scenes: bottle, pour, palm, glow6+

04 · the aesthetic

the aesthetic.

16

nadia lee cohen meets 90s gucci. pretty people in a nice house acting like none of this is strange. warm yellow cast, creamy highlights, lifted blacks, grain, dust and the odd scratch. red, white and cream against a lot of wood.

board pulls for mood. we're borrowing the feeling, not copying anybody's shot.

no smilingserious, aloof, unbothered. no looking at camera unless it's santa.
product in framebottle, box or GLAZE paper in every shot. no exceptions.
grip holdsoiled up and nobody slips. the oil absorbs, it never reads greasy.
red + whiteminimal tree, red and white ornaments only. the house does the rest.

08 · social cuts

the postcards.

08

the social cuts are postcards. one moment each, 6 to 10 seconds, looping clean. 9:16 for stories and reels, 4:5 for the feed. each one opens on its strangest frame, hits a macro of the oil in the middle and ends on the bottle or a box. cream border, a GLAZE stamp in the corner and one short handwritten line, like a holiday card from the house.

postcard frames shown with the ai clips. lines are placeholders, we'll write the final ones with brittni.
