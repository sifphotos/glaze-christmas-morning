01 · project brief

the brief.

01

everyone slept over. santa is in every room. nobody asks why.

christmas morning in a 1966 mid century house. the girls, him, the founder and an old santa who shows up everywhere, deadpan, and never explains himself. he's the old man from the birthday drop, back for the holidays.

no dialogue. nobody smiles at camera. think gucci campaign seriousness and nadia lee cohen. slow-mo, 2.39 widescreen for the film, full vertical for the cutdowns, matte film with grain and dust.

the rules on set: a bottle, a box or the logo paper is in every single frame. the oil always reads as absorbs, grip holds, never greasy. the end card is the GLAZE logo and nothing else.

i'm shooting stills the whole day between takes. run and gun, continuous light with a softened speedlight pop.

  • full film2.39 letterboxed, every room45 – 50s
  • hero cutthe main ad30s
  • cutdownbathroom, den, bar, pull back15s
  • clean ad cutinsert removed, safe to run paid30s
  • postcardsthe social cuts. short 9:16 + 4:5 loops, one moment each8
  • stillsfull scene + close heroes, 4:5 and 9:16~25
  • product heroesshot inside their scenes: bottle, pour, palm, glow6+

03 · the aesthetic

the aesthetic.

16

nadia lee cohen meets 90s gucci. pretty people in a nice house acting like none of this is strange. warm yellow cast, creamy highlights, lifted blacks, grain, dust and the odd scratch. red, white and cream against a lot of wood.

board pulls for mood. we're borrowing the feeling, not copying anybody's shot.

no smilingserious, aloof, unbothered. no looking at camera unless it's santa.
product in framebottle, box or GLAZE paper in every shot. no exceptions.
grip holdsoiled up and nobody slips. the oil absorbs, it never reads greasy.
red + whiteminimal tree, red and white ornaments only. the house does the rest.

06 · story overview

the story.

~48s

we start in the bathroom with her and the bottle, and santa walking past the door. a quick look at him waking up and the girls still asleep tells you who stayed the night. then we just move through the house. every room has somebody in it doing their christmas morning, and santa is somewhere he shouldn't be.

two gifting beats carry it: gift your man (the den) and gift your girls (bedroom 2). two grip beats prove the oil: the arm wrestle and twister. the calvin klein insert sells the waistband.

it all ends in the living room. slow pull back, everyone in their spot, santa center. logo.

  1. open · the bathroom, the pour, santa passes
  2. wake · him, the waistband, a box on the nightstand
  3. the sleepover · the girls asleep, middle of the bed empty

  6. gift your girls · the mauve room, boxes on santa

07 · the edit

the cut.

36s
rough ai edit.

this is a rough cut stitched from the ai storyboard clips, no audio, purely for the proposal. it shows order, pacing and where the macros of the oil land, and that's it. the real film will feel more real and more emotional, with better flow and pacing from shot to shot. nothing here is locked. we can move scenes around as we need to.

track · fortunate, maxwell (slowed + reverb) tempo · about 76 bpm original, roughly 60 slowed, so a beat is about a second audio reference →

08 · social cuts

the postcards.

08

the social cuts are postcards. one moment each, 6 to 10 seconds, looping clean. 9:16 for stories and reels, 4:5 for the feed. each one opens on its strangest frame, hits a macro of the oil in the middle and ends on the bottle or a box. cream border, a GLAZE stamp in the corner and one short handwritten line, like a holiday card from the house.

postcard frames shown with the ai clips. lines are placeholders, we'll write the final ones with brittni.
